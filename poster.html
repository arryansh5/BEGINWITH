📋 Abstract

This project presents a full-stack intelligent system for predicting stock price movement and evaluating IPO investment potential. The system leverages machine learning (Logistic Regression) on technical indicators derived from historical market data to generate next-day price direction predictions.

A complementary rule-based IPO scoring engine rates initial public offerings using market signals — subscription rate, Grey Market Premium (GMP), company profitability, and sector risk.

The unified web application provides retail investors with AI-assisted, data-driven investment insights in real time.
🎯 Introduction & Motivation
  • Retail investors lack access to institutional-grade analytics tools.
  • Stock market is highly volatile; sentiment-based decisions lead to losses.
  • IPO evaluation requires analyzing multiple signals simultaneously.
  • Existing tools are either too complex or require expensive subscriptions.
Objective: Build a free, accessible, full-stack platform that uses ML to guide smarter investment decisions.
  • Uses Yahoo Finance API for real, live market data.
  • Covers both NSE/BSE (India) and NASDAQ/NYSE (US) stocks.

🔬 Methodology

A. Stock Prediction Pipeline:

  • Fetch 2 years of OHLCV data from Yahoo Finance
  • Engineer technical indicators: MA5, MA10, ΔPrice, ΔVolume
  • Binary target: tomorrow's close > today's close → UP (1) else DOWN (0)
  • Train Logistic Regression on scaled 80% data, predict on 20%
  • Output: Prediction (UP/DOWN), Confidence %, Risk Level

B. IPO Evaluation Algorithm:

  • Subscription Rate >20x → +2 pts | 10–20x → +1 pt | <10x → −1 pt
  • GMP% >25% → +2 pts | 10–25% → +1 pt | <10% → −1 pt
  • Company Profitable → +1 pt | Loss-making → −1 pt
  • Strong Sector (Banking/Energy) → +1 pt | Risky → −1 pt
  • Final Rating: Good ≥ 4 pts | Average ≥ 2 pts | Risky < 2 pts
