\begin{tcolorbox}[sbox, title={[1] Abstract}]
\bodyfont
This project presents a full-stack intelligent system for
\textbf{predicting stock price movement} (UP/DOWN) and
\textbf{evaluating IPO investment potential} using Logistic
Regression on 2-year historical OHLCV market data.
\vspace{5pt}

A \textbf{rule-based IPO scoring engine} rates IPOs using:
subscription rate, GMP\%, profitability, and sector risk.
\vspace{5pt}

\begin{tcolorbox}[hbox]
\bodyfont
The unified web app gives retail investors
\textbf{AI-assisted, data-driven insights} in real time.
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{8pt}

\begin{tcolorbox}[sbox, title={[2] Introduction \& Motivation}]
\bodyfont
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=3pt,leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Retail investors lack \textbf{institutional-grade} tools.
  \item Volatility causes \textbf{sentiment-driven losses}.
  \item IPO evaluation needs \textbf{multiple signals}.
  \item Existing platforms are complex or expensive.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{5pt}
\begin{tcolorbox}[hbox]
\bodyfont\textbf{Objective:} Free, accessible ML platform for
smarter investment decisions in real time.
\end{tcolorbox}
\vspace{5pt}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=3pt,leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Live \textbf{Yahoo Finance} via \texttt{yfinance}.
  \item 10+ NSE, BSE, and NASDAQ tickers.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

\vspace{8pt}

\begin{tcolorbox}[sbox, title={[3] Dataset \& Features}]
\bodyfont
\textbf{Source:} \texttt{yfinance} --- 2-year daily OHLCV.
\vspace{6pt}

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\begin{center}
\smallfont
\begin{tabular}{>{\bfseries}l p{9cm}}
\toprule
Feature & Description\\
\midrule
MA5 & 5-day rolling mean of Close\\
MA10 & 10-day rolling mean of Close\\
Price\_Change & $\Delta C_t = C_t - C_{t-1}$\\
Volume\_Change & $\Delta V_t = V_t - V_{t-1}$\\

Target $y$ & 1=UP, 0=DOWN (next day)\\

\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep,topsep=3pt,leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item Drop NaN rows after rolling windows
  \item \textbf{StandardScaler} normalization
  \item \textbf{80/20} chronological split
\end{enumerate}

\begin{tcolorbox}[sbox, title={[6] Methodology}]
\bodyfont
\textbf{A. Stock Prediction:}
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep,topsep=3pt,leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item Fetch 2yr OHLCV via \texttt{yfinance}
  \item Compute MA5, MA10, $\Delta$Price, $\Delta$Volume
  \item Target: close$_{t+1}>$close$_t \Rightarrow y=1$
  \item Normalize with \texttt{StandardScaler}
  \item Train \textbf{Logistic Regression} on 80\% data
  \item Return: Prediction, Confidence\%, Risk Level
\end{enumerate}
\vspace{5pt}
\textbf{B. IPO Scoring:}
\vspace{3pt}

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\begin{center}
\smallfont
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
\textbf{Signal} & \textbf{Condition} & \textbf{Pts}\\
\midrule
Sub Rate & $>20\times/10$--$20\times/<10\times$ & $+2/+1/-1$\\
GMP\%    & $>25\%/10$--$25\%/<10\%$ & $+2/+1/-1$\\
Profit   & Profitable / Loss & $+1/-1$\\
Sector   & Strong/Neutral/Risky & $+1/0/-1$\\
\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Rating}} & $\ge4$:Good $\ge2$:Avg $<2$:Risky\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\end{tcolorbox}

\textbf{Learned Model Weights} (RELIANCE.NS sample):
\vspace{3pt}

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\begin{center}
\smallfont
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
\textbf{Feature} & \textbf{Weight ($\beta$)} & \textbf{Effect}\\
\midrule
Intercept       & $+0.0624$ & Base UP bias\\
MA5             & $-0.3868$ & High MA5 $\to$ DOWN\\
MA10            & $+0.2565$ & High MA10 $\to$ UP\\
Price\_Change   & $-0.0073$ & Weak momentum\\
Volume\_Change  & $+0.1292$ & Rising vol $\to$ UP\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\vspace{4pt}
\begin{tcolorbox}[hbox]
\bodyfont\textbf{Key:} MA5 is always $-$ve (mean-reversion),
MA10 always $+$ve (trend-following). Weights differ per ticker.
